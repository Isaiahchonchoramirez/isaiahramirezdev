# Paid pilot offer

> Commercial draft. Confidentiality, data-processing, limitation-of-liability,
> professional-services boundaries, and jurisdictional language require legal review
> before use with customer data.

## Exact customer profile

An active self-funded or traditional searcher, independent sponsor, or two-to-five-
person acquisition team under LOI for a privately held U.S. small business. The buyer
has authority to procure the service and share a bounded package. The initial hypothesis
is $2M–$25M enterprise value; validation may narrow it.

## Qualifying project

- One approved buyer-side request list.
- 300–1,500 files, no more than 20 GB or 15,000 pages.
- At least one PDF document set and one CSV/XLSX operating dataset.
- One named customer reviewer.
- One baseline package and one optional update of no more than 20% new/changed files.
- Excludes public-company, patient, cardholder, credential, export-controlled,
  unauthorized privileged, and other prohibited data.

## What Reef delivers

Within the agreed scope:

1. immutable package inventory and exception list;
2. processing and coverage appendix;
3. customer-confirmed request-list coverage matrix;
4. deterministic diagnostics for up to five supported structured datasets;
5. up to ten pre-agreed cross-source reconciliation checks;
6. human-reviewed evidence register with exact source anchors and finding classes;
7. one baseline-to-update delta within the revision allowance;
8. 60-minute customer review and corrected final deliverable;
9. retention/deletion confirmation under the agreement.

## Turnaround

Standard target: five business days after complete authorized intake and customer
answers to classification questions. Inventory draft arrives within two business days.
The clock pauses for missing access, passwords, scope decisions, or prohibited data.

## Customer responsibilities

- Warrant authority to disclose and identify restricted categories.
- Obtain required seller, VDR, investor, counsel, or other approvals.
- Supply the approved request list, package boundary, definitions, periods, and named
  reviewer.
- Keep counsel, accountants, tax, QoE, and other specialists engaged.
- Confirm request mappings and missing/not-applicable states.
- Review all findings before external reliance.
- Pay deposit and balance under the accepted experiment.

## What Reef does not do

Reef does not provide legal, accounting, audit, tax, quality-of-earnings, valuation,
commercial-market, investment, fiduciary, lender, insurance, cybersecurity, technical,
or transaction-approval services. Reef does not certify completeness, accuracy,
compliance, solvency, value, or readiness to close. Reef reviews only the supplied,
processable, agreed scope.

## Confidentiality and data handling

Contract language must be reviewed by qualified counsel before use. The commercial
draft should require mutual confidentiality, purpose limitation, minimum access,
approved subprocessors, encryption, incident notification, no model training on
customer data, no undisclosed retention, return/deletion, and a clear statement about
backup expiry.

The customer must not send content until the actual transfer method, storage, access,
retention, and deletion controls match the agreement. Reef must refuse work requiring
controls it cannot demonstrate.

## Pricing experiments

Each offer is a test. Do not discount to free. Record offer, response, approval process,
deposit date, and reason.

### Experiment A: paid design-partner pilot

- **Price:** $1,500 fixed.
- **Deposit:** 50% at signature; non-refundable after authorized intake begins, except
  Reef cancels because it cannot safely process the agreed package.
- **Scope:** up to 500 files, 5 GB, 5,000 pages, two datasets, five checks, one update of
  up to 10%.
- **Customer consideration:** two workflow interviews, artifact feedback, measured
  baseline, outcome review, and permission to retain de-identified metrics. No public
  logo or quote without separate approval.
- **Purpose:** prove payment and workflow, not maximize revenue.

### Experiment B: standard fixed-package review

- **Price:** $4,500 fixed.
- **Deposit:** 50% at signature; balance due on draft delivery before final export.
- **Scope:** up to 1,500 files, 20 GB, 15,000 pages, five datasets, ten checks, one update
  of up to 20%.
- **Turnaround:** five business days after complete intake.
- **Purpose:** test sustainable package economics and buyer approval.

### Experiment C: premium rush or high-complexity review

- **Price:** $8,500 fixed after intake qualification.
- **Deposit:** 60% at signature; balance due on draft delivery.
- **Scope:** standard boundaries, either two-business-day rush or approved complexity
  such as poor OCR/additional mappings; never both without written rescope.
- **Turnaround:** two business days for rush, subject to immediate customer responses.
- **Purpose:** test urgency premium and operational limits.

Fees exclude specialist advisors, source-system charges, extraordinary conversion,
travel, sales/use tax, and work outside written scope.

## Revision allowance

One update within the tier limit is included if received within seven calendar days of
draft delivery. Further changes require a written fixed-price amendment. A “revision”
is changed source material, not unlimited edits to conclusions.

Reef corrects factual transcription or anchor errors without charge. New scope,
definitions, checks, datasets, or advisor questions are change requests.

## Cancellation

- Customer may cancel before intake; deposit is returned less non-recoverable agreed
  setup costs, if any.
- After intake starts, deposit is non-refundable because capacity and handling work are
  committed.
- Reef may stop and return the unearned portion if authority is unclear, prohibited data
  is supplied, required controls are unavailable, scope is materially misrepresented,
  payment fails, or work would cross professional boundaries.
- Data return/deletion duties survive cancellation.

Final contract terms require legal review and may differ.

## Measurable success criteria

The pilot succeeds only if:

- customer baseline and Reef hands-on time are recorded for covered tasks;
- covered customer review time falls by at least 30%;
- every factual deliverable item has an exact source anchor;
- at least 95% of sampled anchors are correct;
- customer accepts at least 80% of shipped deterministic/direct-fact findings, with
  check-level precision meeting the scorecard;
- the deliverable is used in an advisor escalation, diligence meeting, investor update,
  or equivalent real workflow;
- customer pays the balance;
- customer commits to a repeat deal, design-partner continuation, or qualified referral.

Customer satisfaction alone is not success.

## Accuracy guarantee

Adopted by ADR-002 from the alternative package, replacing a satisfaction guarantee.

> Full refund, including deposit, if the delivered register contains a **factual error in
> any finding classified Critical or High** — an anchor that does not support the claim,
> a misquoted source, or a miscalculated figure.

Not a refund for dissatisfaction, scope disagreement, or findings the customer already
knew. A satisfaction guarantee invites negotiation about value. An accuracy guarantee
states the product thesis as a contractual term and is falsifiable by inspection.

Reef corrects transcription and anchor errors without charge in every case; the refund
applies where an error reached the customer in a Critical or High finding.

## Outreach

Sent only to prospects with an active LOI or an active mandate. No product language.

> You mentioned you're in diligence on [deal]. I'm running paid pilots this month: I take
> the room and the request list and come back with an inventory, a coverage matrix
> showing what's actually been supplied against what you asked for, a profile of the
> operating data, and an issue register where every line links to the page or cell it
> came from.
>
> Five business days, $4,500 — or $1,500 as a design partner if you're willing to do two
> workflow interviews and let me measure the baseline. It's me doing the work, not
> software, and it doesn't replace your attorney or your QoE. It's the reconciliation
> you'd otherwise do yourself.
>
> Want a slot?

Naming the price in the first message is deliberate. It disqualifies fast, and the
reaction to the number is itself the finding (`PAY1`, `PAY2`).

The line about doing it yourself should be replaced with a verbatim participant quote as
soon as one exists.
