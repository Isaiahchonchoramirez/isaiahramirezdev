# Mission

## The sentence

**Make consequential information understandable, connected, and verifiable before
people act on it.**

“Upload anything. Understand everything.” remains a long-term expression, not the first
product specification. It does not identify a buyer, deadline, deliverable, or reason
to pay.

## The first concrete mission

For an active searcher or independent sponsor, Reef turns a bounded buyer-side data
room and request list into a human-reviewed evidence register showing:

- what was supplied and what could not be processed;
- which requested items appear absent or incomplete;
- which defined facts conflict across supplied sources;
- what structured operating data contains duplicates, gaps, type problems, or material
  concentration patterns;
- which questions remain unresolved and should be escalated to the appropriate advisor.

Every factual finding records the originating document and exact page, section, table,
sheet, row, or text span. Reef does not provide legal, tax, accounting, valuation,
quality-of-earnings, investment, or transaction-approval advice.

## Outcomes, not activity

Reef succeeds when it changes the diligence workflow:

- the buyer spends less time inventorying and searching;
- missing requested material is surfaced earlier;
- defined inconsistencies reach the correct specialist with evidence attached;
- investor/advisor updates are assembled from a defensible issue register;
- updates to the room do not force a complete manual reread.

Uploads, embeddings, graph nodes, questions, and generated summaries are implementation
activity, not mission outcomes.

## Validation mission tests

A proposed concierge activity belongs only when all are true:

1. It helps an active small-cap buyer complete a real diligence task.
2. It reduces manual review time or exposes a meaningful gap or inconsistency.
3. Its factual output can be tied to exact supplied evidence.
4. A qualified buyer can judge its usefulness in one live or realistic project.
5. It stays outside reserved professional advice and final deal judgment.

## Initial target measures

| Measure | Pass target | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Time to first inventory | under 1 business day | The service must fit an active deal |
| Factual citation coverage | 100% | Uncited facts cannot enter the deliverable |
| Citation-anchor correctness | at least 95% | Reviewers must land on the claimed evidence |
| Shipped deterministic finding precision | at least 95% | Noise destroys trust |
| Missing-evidence precision | at least 90% after customer confirms the request list | “Missing” is costly if scope is wrong |
| Customer review time saved | at least 30% for covered tasks | The economics must be visible |
| Paid validation | at least two pilots at or above the design-partner price | Compliments are not demand |

Targets are hypotheses until pilots establish baselines. Revisions require evidence and
an ADR update.

## Product promise

Reef does not promise complete diligence or a correct transaction decision. It promises
to perform a disclosed set of inventory, evidence, gap, reconciliation, and structured-
data checks consistently; show the source trail; expose uncertainty; and preserve the
review record for the customer's professional team.
