# Reef Labs

> Status: canonical company direction. Market authority comes from
> [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001-initial-market-wedge.md); implementation remains blocked
> by the [validation scorecard](../validation/SCORECARD.md).

Reef Labs builds evidence systems for consequential reviews. Its first validation
wedge is buyer-side diligence for searchers and independent sponsors acquiring small
businesses. Reef organizes a supplied data room, identifies missing requested material,
profiles structured operating data, reconciles defined facts, and produces a human-
reviewed issue register in which every factual claim links to exact evidence.

The company is not starting as a universal knowledge platform or an automated deal
advisor. It starts with one bounded question: **can a small acquisition team understand
what is present, missing, inconsistent, and worth escalating without losing the source
trail?**

## Mission

Make consequential information understandable, connected, and verifiable before
people act on it.

See [MISSION.md](MISSION.md) for operating measures and product limits.

## Vision

Organizations should be able to ask what they know, what changed, what conflicts, and
what is missing, then inspect the evidence behind every answer. Over time, Reef may
become an understanding layer between systems that store information and people who
must make decisions from it. The M&A wedge earns or disproves that direction; it does
not guarantee it.

## Core values

### Evidence before eloquence

A fluent answer without traceable evidence is a liability. Reef shows the page,
paragraph, table cell, sheet, row, or source record behind every material factual claim.

### Ruthless focus

When ideas compete, choose the one that gets a real customer to pay sooner while
preserving long-term options. One exceptional workflow earns the right to expand.

### Respect accountable professionals

Reef supports the buyer, counsel, accountants, tax advisors, lenders, and other
specialists; it does not impersonate them. It exposes uncertainty and leaves judgment
with the people retained and accountable for it.

### Ship the small true thing

Unsupported formats, missing evidence, weak confidence, and unresolved ambiguity are
explicit states. A short verified report beats a broad synthetic answer.

### Calm craft

Speed, accessibility, and clarity come before spectacle. Beauty reduces cognitive load
and makes evidence easier to navigate.

### Earn expansion

New modalities, buyer segments, infrastructure, and enterprise controls respond to
observed constraints. Architecture preserves options without paying every future cost.

## Company direction

| Horizon | Company hypothesis | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Validation | Paid manual diligence evidence service | Repeated pain, governed package access, two paid pilots, useful deliverables |
| Initial product | Evidence-linked data-room review for small acquisition teams | Scorecard passes and M1 is authorized by a new ADR |
| Expansion | Repeatable sponsor, advisor, and portfolio review | Repeat deals, referrals, and recurring account usage |
| Platform horizon | Evidence infrastructure for other consequential reviews | More than one retained workflow shares stable primitives |

Each horizon must be paid for and validated by the one before it.

## What Reef Labs will not become

- A generic chatbot with an ocean-themed wrapper.
- A law firm, accounting firm, investment advisor, valuation provider, or quality-of-
  earnings provider.
- A system that says a deal is good, safe, compliant, or ready to close.
- A system that hides uncertain output behind confident prose.
- A speculative platform built for enterprise requirements no named customer requests.
