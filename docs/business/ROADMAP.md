# Company roadmap

The roadmap advances through evidence gates. Current authority extends only through
validation and manual paid pilots.

## V0: reconciliation and research setup

**Complete when:** ADR-001, one canonical index, historical banners, hypotheses,
interview guide, workflow, pilot plan, synthetic specification, offer, scorecard, and
research log are committed without unrelated work.

This phase does not authorize application code.

## V1: 15 qualified interviews

**Goal:** determine who owns request-list reconciliation, how much time and consequence
it creates, what tools already solve, who can buy, and whether packages can be shared.

- At least 8 searchers/sponsors.
- At least 4 advisor, investor, accounting, counsel, or VDR roles.
- Every interview reconstructs a recent real transaction.
- Commitment ladder tests redacted data, test participation, introductions, agreement,
  and payment.

**Exit:** interview, pain, access, and commitment rows in the scorecard are Pass. If
they fail, narrow, pivot to engineering, or stop.

## V2: synthetic operations calibration

**Goal:** make the manual method reproducible and test evidence mechanics safely.

- Generate the specified fictional package in a separate future task.
- Build gold manifest, finding, negative-control, and revision-delta ledgers.
- Rehearse intake, inventory, request mapping, structured profiling, reconciliations,
  evidence anchors, report, and deletion.
- Have a transaction professional review wording and scope.

**Exit:** synthetic technical thresholds pass. This phase never substitutes for
customer packages or payments.

## V3: governed sample packages

**Goal:** learn actual format, naming, version, request-list, and security constraints.

- At least three distinct authorized redacted packages.
- Record only approved format/volume and evaluation artifacts.
- Identify five or fewer repeatable first checks.
- Test citation anchors and request mappings against human review.

**Exit:** data-access, format, security, evidence, and precision rows pass.

## V4: paid concierge pilots

**Goal:** prove purchase and real workflow value before product implementation.

- Present at least five qualified written offers.
- Complete at least two paid pilots with 50% deposits.
- Deliver within standard boundaries and record operator cost.
- Observe whether the register enters an advisor/investor workflow.
- Test repeat/referral/channel commitment and a standard-price path.

**Exit:** payment, time-saving, turnaround, real-use, repeatability, professional-
boundary, referral, and competitive-displacement rows pass.

## V5: ADR-002

Review all supportive and disconfirming evidence. Choose exactly one:

1. build a narrow M1;
2. narrow the M&A workflow and revalidate;
3. pivot validation to engineering technical-package assurance;
4. stop Reef.

Only option 1 authorizes application implementation. ADR-002 must name the exact input,
user, checks, output, security boundary, evaluation set, and exit criteria.

## M1: evidence substrate, if authorized

Potential scope is defined in [MVP.md](../product/MVP.md), but it remains dormant until
ADR-002. Architecture begins with stable source anchors, immutable manifests, tenant
isolation, resumable processing, and a human review surface, not a broad chat product.

## Later hypotheses, not roadmap commitments

- One package update and affected-register comparison.
- Sponsor/advisor multi-project capacity.
- Controlled VDR or storage integrations.
- Team dispositions, comments, and report history.
- Post-close evidence continuity.
- Ocean corpus map only after flat workflows retain.
- Engineering wedge only through a new ADR and its own validation cycle.

## Roadmap rules

1. No M1 before the exact scorecard and ADR gate.
2. No free pilot counts as demand.
3. No live data before authority and counsel-reviewed terms.
4. No professional conclusion enters Reef's service.
5. No new modality without a paid workflow requiring it.
6. No enterprise control without a named blocker or baseline security necessity.
7. No infrastructure graduation without measured pressure.
8. Negative evidence can kill the wedge; documents do not outrank customers.
