# Business model

## What Reef sells

Reef's validation offer sells manual evidence preparation for a bounded buyer-side
acquisition package. The customer is not buying tokens, generic storage, a complete
diligence opinion, or a chatbot. The customer is buying faster inventory and request
reconciliation, structured-data diagnostics, defined fact checks, exact citations, and
a reviewed issue register for their professional team.

No recurring business model is assumed until buyer behavior proves one.

## Paid validation motion

Use the three experiments in [PILOT_OFFER.md](../validation/PILOT_OFFER.md):

| Experiment | Price | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Paid design partner | $1,500 | Prove commitment, workflow, data access, and output |
| Standard fixed package | $4,500 | Test sustainable package economics |
| Rush/high complexity | $8,500 | Test urgency premium and operating boundaries |

Deposits are required. Free work may be used only for synthetic calibration, never as
willingness-to-pay evidence.

## Potential product pricing after M1

Not yet decided. Candidate models include per-project review, sponsor/advisor annual
capacity, or software plus reviewed-service tiers. Seat pricing may be dishonest for an
episodic searcher. Token or “AI credit” pricing is rejected because it is unpredictable
and encourages incomplete packages.

A pricing decision requires paid pilot cost, repeat behavior, project frequency,
security/support overhead, and channel evidence.

## Value model

For every pilot calculate from observed task data:

```text
covered buyer hours avoided
+ repeated-work hours avoided after an update
+ documented advisor preparation saved
+ customer-valued consequence of an accepted gap found earlier
- pilot fee
- customer intake and review time
= measured customer value
```

Do not assign speculative value to a deal risk or market Reef as preventing a loss that
the customer did not measure.

## Route to customer

Founder-led research and sales begin in concentrated ETA communities, then test trusted
channels: transaction counsel, QoE providers, brokers, lenders, and investors. The best
channel repeatedly encounters qualified packages and benefits from better-prepared
evidence without viewing Reef as unauthorized competition.

## Recurrence hypotheses

1. A searcher may use Reef on more than one serious target.
2. An independent sponsor or small fund may run several reviews each year.
3. A boutique advisor or provider may refer or purchase capacity across clients.
4. Room-update comparison may create repeated use within one deal.
5. Evidence may support post-close integration, but that is outside validation scope.

If paid pilots produce no repeat, referral, or advisor-channel signal, Reef should not
pretend a per-deal project is SaaS. Engineering becomes a stronger reversal candidate.

## Gross-margin discipline

Record founder/operator hours, specialist-review hours, storage, extraction/OCR, model,
security, support, and deletion costs per package. By pilot two, non-repeatable expert
work must be under 20 hours/package for the M1 gate.

Deterministic checks run before generative inference. Immutable extraction and unchanged
documents can be reused only within the authorized retention boundary.

## Commercial and legal risks

- VDR incumbents and general assistants already provide cited review functions.
- Buyers may not trust a new operator with deal data.
- Useful work may require professional judgment Reef cannot safely sell.
- Searchers may be too episodic or price-sensitive for acceptable acquisition economics.
- Security controls may make manual delivery slower and more expensive than assumed.
- Services may remain bespoke and resist productization.

The scorecard converts these into stop conditions.

## Company metrics before M1

- Qualified interview and artifact conversion.
- Governed package access.
- Signed offers, deposits, payments, and time to approval.
- Baseline and Reef time by covered task.
- Finding acceptance/rejection and evidence-anchor accuracy.
- Deliverable use outside the Reef review call.
- Repeat-project and qualified-referral commitments.
- Operator hours and direct cost per package.

Signups, uploaded-file counts, and compliments are not validation metrics.
