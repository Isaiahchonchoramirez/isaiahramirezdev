# Customer profiles

> These are recruiting hypotheses governed by [ADR-001](../decisions/ADR-001-initial-market-wedge.md), not validated personas.

## Initial profile

The first candidate is an active searcher or independent sponsor under LOI for a
privately held U.S. small business. The team is one to five people, personally manages
the data room and advisor handoffs, and can authorize a paid bounded service.

Qualifying signals:

- active or completed transaction in the last 24 months;
- buyer-side request list and a package containing documents plus structured data;
- hands-on responsibility for inventory, request coverage, data checks, or issue register;
- fixed diligence deadline;
- authority to share a governed redacted subset and procure a pilot;
- specialist advisors remain engaged for professional scopes.

Initial exclusions include public-company transactions, healthcare rooms containing
patient data, regulated financial institutions, export-controlled content, cross-border
complexity requiring jurisdiction-specific handling, and buyers who cannot authorize a
lawful package export.

## Primary user and economic buyer: searcher

**Job:** acquire one viable business while coordinating investors, lenders, seller,
management, and specialists with limited internal staff.

**Candidate pain:** personally inventories an inconsistent room, maintains a request
tracker, profiles operating data, searches for supporting facts, and turns advisor work
into an investor-ready issue list under exclusivity pressure.

**Success:** less time on covered preparation, earlier gaps, exact evidence for
escalation, and no false confidence about what Reef did not review.

**Objections:** confidentiality, unknown-founder trust, advisor overlap, episodic value,
false alarms, data leaving the VDR, and unclear professional boundaries.

## Expansion candidate: independent sponsor or small-fund principal

This buyer may run several diligence processes and support recurring revenue. Validate:

- annual qualified deal frequency;
- repeatable internal checklists and analyst work;
- ability to standardize without erasing deal differences;
- procurement/security approval;
- value of room-update comparison and portfolio reuse.

Do not promote this profile to initial buyer merely because recurring revenue looks
better on paper.

## Channel candidates

### Boutique M&A advisor or broker

Potential source of repeated introductions and sell-side room-quality insight. Risk:
Reef may conflict with the advisor's paid scope or create client liability.

### Transaction counsel

Boundary and evidence expert, not necessarily purchaser. Counsel must help define what a
non-lawyer evidence service may prepare and which content is privileged or unsafe.

### QoE accountant or financial diligence provider

Can distinguish commodity data preparation from professional accounting judgment and
assess whether DataGate-derived diagnostics save time or duplicate scope.

### Investor or lender

Consumer of buyer evidence and source of approval requirements. May reveal what must be
supported before a claim affects a decision.

### VDR administrator or deal operations lead

Understands export permissions, version metadata, native AI functions, and where an
external service loses source context.

## Design-partner requirements

The strongest partner has a qualified project within 30 days, written package authority,
a request list, at least one structured dataset, a named reviewer, willingness to
measure baseline hours, and authority to pay at least $1,500 with a 50% deposit.

Avoid innovation-only contacts, people without recent deal responsibility, requests for
free speculative work, and participants who cannot obtain data authorization.

## Recruiting channels

- Searchfunder and university entrepreneurship-through-acquisition communities.
- Local and regional ETA groups.
- SBA acquisition lenders, transaction attorneys, QoE providers, brokers, and investor
  networks, approached for research rather than implied endorsement.
- Qualified warm introductions from completed interviews.

Public membership counts prove channel existence, not response or conversion.

## Interview standard

Use [INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md](../validation/INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md). Ask for the most recent real
workflow, artifact, time, failure, and approval path. Interest without a dated artifact,
introduction, signed agreement, deposit, or payment is not validation.
