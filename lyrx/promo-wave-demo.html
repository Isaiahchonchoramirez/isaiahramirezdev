lyrx-wave — live canvas vs the static plate

Dozens of strands, a bloom pass, and vertical spectrum columns, keyed blue to violet with the brand lime threaded through every seventh strand. It pauses when it scrolls offscreen and renders one static frame when the visitor asks for reduced motion. The field is drawn into an offscreen buffer so the glow is a single blur rather than a shadow on each of ~340 strokes — that one change took it from 4fps to 49.

Side by side

waveform.png at 23% — 1.1MB, and it never moves.

<lyrx-wave> — 8KB, and it breathes.

Amplitude and spread

amp="0.45" spread="0.14" — a tight band to sit behind copy.

amp="1.5" spread="0.5" — fills the frame, works as a divider.

Density, spikes and glow

density="14" spikes="60" glow="0.6" — sparse and quiet.

density="72" spikes="380" glow="2" — as dense as it goes.

The dark wash

Default — paints its own near-black wash over whatever is behind it.

background="transparent" — the violet plate shows through. Use this whenever the element overlays page content.
