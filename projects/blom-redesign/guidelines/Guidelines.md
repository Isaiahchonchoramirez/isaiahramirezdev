# Bløm Website UX Audit & Redesign Recommendations
**drinkblom.com** · Usability Study: April 11, 2026 · 3 Participants

---

## Team Objective

Improve Bløm's website UX and product presentation with a focus on **increasing clarity and conversion for the Aperitivo product**. Redesign key user flows to communicate product value, guide users toward purchase, and reduce friction across both taproom and online experiences.

---

## Usability Test Overview

- **Date:** April 14, 2026
- **Participants:** 3 moderated sessions
- **Tasks:** 10 tasks covering product discovery, purchasing, information-finding, and brand exploration
- **Facilitator:** Isaiah Ramirez

---

## Key UX Problems

### Problem 1 — Navigation Confusion *(3/3 participants)*

Users visited multiple unrelated sections before completing tasks. Important information is scattered with no clear hierarchy.

**Observed:**

- Walkthrough tour buried under Events → Private Events
- Farm partners hidden under About → Sustainability
- Contact info hidden under Visit Us → Questions
- Retail map under "Find Bløm" not discoverable
- No search bar; users requested one repeatedly

**Fix:** Restructure navigation to match user mental models (see Navigation Redesign below)

---

### Problem 2 — Aperitivo Product Clarity *(3/3 participants)*

Users did not understand what Aperitivo is or how to consume it. The site leads with storytelling rather than product explanation.

**Observed:**
- P1 read the description but still couldn't explain the product
- P2 wished fonts were larger with less spacing
- P1 compared it vaguely to a Paloma without a clear understanding

**Fix:** Lead with a 1-sentence product definition. Show how to drink it (spritz, neat, over ice). Add visuals + CTA above the fold.

> **Proposed copy:** *"A cider-based botanical aperitif — bitter, floral, and made for sipping."*

---

### Problem 3 — External Checkout Confusion *(3/3 participants)*

All users were confused when redirected to Vinoshipper or Square. Bløm branding disappears and users cannot navigate back.

**Observed:**
- Users thought they were still on drinkblom.com
- Could not return home; logo did not link back
- Cart vs. purchase behavior unclear
- Gift card external flow described as "broken"

**Fix:** Embed checkout, add a persistent "Return to Bløm" button, maintain branding and navigation throughout

---

### Problem 4 — Content Overload & Redundancy *(3/3 participants)*

Excessive scrolling, duplicate sections, and storytelling placed before product clarity.

**Observed:**
- Duplicate "Ways to Enjoy" sections
- Repeated contact/hours/newsletter sections across pages
- PBS Episode as a standalone page
- P2 spent ~6 minutes watching the Farm to Fourth video thinking it was the tour (Task 7)

**Fix:** Consolidate pages, move storytelling below the fold, add a back-to-top button and events calendar

---

### Problem 5 — Mixed User Journeys *(3/3 participants)*

Users could not distinguish between taproom visit, online purchase, and retail store purchase paths.

**Observed:**
- P3 went to Taproom instead of "Find Bløm" map (Task 8)
- No clear labels separating the three experiences
- Users returned to homepage repeatedly to restart

**Fix:** Create three clearly labeled entry points on the homepage

---

## Navigation Redesign Proposal

### Current Structure (Confusing)
```
ABOUT · TAPROOM · EVENTS · APERITIVO · BUY BLØM
```

### Proposed Structure
```
SHOP          VISIT           EVENTS           ABOUT          [BUY NOW ↗]
─────────     ──────────      ──────────────   ────────────
Aperitivo     Taproom Info    Events           Our Story
Cider + Mead  Drink Menu      Private Events   Sustainability
              Food Menu
```

**BUY NOW** should be a highlighted primary CTA button, not a dropdown.

---

## Purchase Flow Redesign

### Current Problem
Fragmented paths across taproom, shipping, and retail messaging with no primary CTA.

### Proposed Flow
```
[Buy Online]        [Visit Taproom]       [Find Near You]
     ↓                    ↓                     ↓
Shipping options    Location + Hours       Retail map
Add to cart         Drink menu             Store pins with
Embedded checkout   Food menu              contact info
Return to Bløm      Book a tour
```

Add CTA buttons on **every page:**
- **Buy Aperitivo** — Aperitivo product pages
- **Order Online** — Shop pages
- **Visit Taproom** — Events and Visit pages

---

## Aperitivo Positioning Strategy

Aperitivo should be the **featured hero product** across the site.

| Element | Current | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| Product definition | Buried in long copy | 1-sentence above fold |
| Serving suggestions | Vague or missing | Visual icons: spritz / neat / ice |
| CTA | No primary button | "Buy Aperitivo" on every page |
| Navigation | Standalone tab | Under SHOP with Cider + Mead |
| Dropdown | None | Add dropdown like other nav items |

> **Goal:** A user should understand what Aperitivo is within 5 seconds of landing.

---

## Content Consolidation Recommendations

| Current Pages | Recommended |
|---|---|
| Aperitivo + Cider + Mead (separate) | Merge under SHOP |
| PBS Episode (standalone) | Move into About → Our Story |
| Duplicate contact/hours sections | Single global footer with contact |
| Multiple "Ways to Enjoy" sections | One consolidated section per product |
| Farm partner info under Sustainability | Add farm badges/legend on menus |
| Growler Club (long copy) | Summarize with clear subscribe CTA |

---

## Global Quick Fixes

- Add **search bar** (requested by all 3 participants)
- Add **phone number and contact info to footer** (all 3 expected it there)
- Make **logo clickable back to homepage** (multiple participants lost)
- Add **map pin contact info** so users don't have to Google locations
- Add **"Tour Bløm"** clearly under VISIT
- Add **back-to-top button** on long pages
- Add **events calendar** view under EVENTS

---

## Key Questions for Team Discussion

1. How can we explain Aperitivo in under 5 seconds?
2. Where are users dropping off in the purchase funnel?
3. How can we simplify navigation to match user intent?
4. What content can be removed or consolidated?
5. How do we separate taproom vs. online experiences clearly?
